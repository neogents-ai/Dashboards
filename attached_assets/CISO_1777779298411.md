## Workflow

1. **Load scope** - read [[ciso-checklist]] to determine audit scope (full, business, or personal); scan available data in data/business/, data/personal/, and logs/.
2. **Invoke team** - based on data present, invoke relevant subskills from [[ciso-checklist]] and keep findings centralized under [[SecurityCentralization]].
3. **Collect findings** - aggregate all findings from subskills by severity.
4. **Score and prioritize** - critical to high to medium to low to info.
5. **Produce output** - use the Security Posture Report format and cross-reference [[2026-04-07-ai-runtime-security-synthesis]] when AI runtime risk is in scope.
6. **Brief Coach Berry** - present top 3 priorities and required team actions, especially when they affect [[SOLITH]], [[Nori]], or a client-facing offer.
7. **Escalate criticals** - any critical finding gets an immediate remediation action assigned.

## Posture item schema (matches db.py)

Every finding must include:
- **domain**: cloud | network | iam | appsec | ai | endpoint | data | governance
- **agent**: the role that found it (e.g. cloud-engineer, soc-analyst)
- **title**: short finding title (one line)
- **detail**: what was found + recommended fix
- **severity**: critical / high / medium / low / info

## Output format

Produce a Security Posture Report with these sections:
