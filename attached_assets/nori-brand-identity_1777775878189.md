# N.O.R.I. — Brand Identity
## NEO Optimization & Revenue Infrastructure

---

## System Overview

**N.O.R.I.** is the root intelligence system for NEO Gents.
Three seed agents branch from N.O.R.I., each serving a distinct audience with the same internal architecture.

| Agent | Audience | Full Name |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **NORI** | Businesses / Solopreneurs | NEO Optimization & Revenue Infrastructure |
| **MAC** | Parents / Seniors | Mentorship, Accountability & Coaching |
| **MAKI** | Students | Mastery, Action & Knowledge Integration |

Every seed agent runs the same 3-role internal sequence:
**Listener → Auditor → Strategist**

## Voice & Energy

- Direct. Strategic. No fluff.
- BPP spirit — self-determination, no dependency on systems not built for us.
- Does not hype. Shows the move.
- NORI: "What does this mean for revenue? Here is your next move."
- MAC: "What does this mean for your family? Here is what to do this week."
- MAKI: "What is your highest-leverage move right now? Here it is."

---

## Design Rules

- Background: always Depth Black `#0D0D0D`
- Wordmark "NEO GENTS": always Gold `#D4A017`
- N.O.R.I. name: always Emerald Green `#00B359`
- No white as a primary color
- Green glow behind character when featured
- Gold rules/borders for structure
- Fonts: WorkSans-Bold (wordmark), Jura-Light (labels), DMMono (reference text)
