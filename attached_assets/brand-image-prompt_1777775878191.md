## Canva Instructions

1. Go to canva.com → Create new design → Custom size: 1080x1080px
2. Upload your generated eye image as the background
3. Add text: **Madam Agency**
4. Font options (all free on Canva):
   - Playfair Display — elegant serif, feels luxury
   - Cormorant Garamond — refined, editorial
   - Bodoni FLF — high fashion, bold
5. Text color: white or very light cream (#F5F0E8) — contrasts against the burgundy fade
6. Position: centered below or over the eyes, or bottom third
7. Optional: add a subtle burgundy color overlay layer at 20-30% opacity to tie it together
